chore(scratch): keep the investigation probes and say what each one proved

These are the one-off diagnostics from the 2026-10-19/21 rounds. They are not imported by the
product, not collected by pytest (2313 tests collected, none from here), and never run in CI --
their only value is that the next person can re-run a measurement instead of re-deriving it.

Keeping them without a label is worse than deleting them: forty-odd unlabelled scripts read as
noise, and the ones that actually carry a conclusion get lost among them. So scratch/README.md
groups them by what they proved, and names the eight that back a real finding -- most importantly
probe_rewrite_variance.py, which is the evidence that real-020's flip comes from the query rewrite
rather than from retrieval, the passage judge or the reranker.

Also fixes the five lint errors that lived here, so `ruff check .` is honest for the whole tree
instead of only for the product paths. That distinction was itself a pitfall last round: a red
ruff that only reflects gitignored scratch/ reads like a broken change.

Two scripts graduated out of here already: scripts/verify_rewrite_stability.py (this round) and
scripts/verify_structured_query_live.py (last round).
